Startup life sells the launch. What actually teaches you is the quiet month after the first “almost.”

I write here about the startup world as I have lived it: SaaS, deeptech, edtech, grants, and the slow craft of seeing a product clearly. Sometimes that means events worth attending. Sometimes it means admitting a hypothesis died.

If you are just entering this world — student, career switcher, curious builder — you are the audience I keep in mind. If you already know the map, you might still want a second set of eyes. Both are welcome.
